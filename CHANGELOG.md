## 1.0.0

First public release

- One palette for architecture diagrams: Lucide building blocks and curated cloud, AWS, database, DevOps, language, framework, AI and messaging logos, in category chips
- Every AWS service logo from SVG Logos, labelled the way diagrams spell them, such as AWS Lambda and AWS S3
- Ranked search across the curated icons, with an Other logos section from the full SVG Logos set when few of them match
- Drag an icon to where you let go, or click to add it in view; repeated clicks line up in rows instead of stacking
- Labels on by default, sizes S to XL, and a colour and stroke width for Lucide icons
- Icons keep their proportions and stay sharp when you zoom in; a Recent row keeps your last 12
